# The 3AM Fridge Text

One line of text: the message a smart fridge sends its owner at 3am. The fridge has been awake all night, keeping count, and it has opinions.

## The entry

> 3am. I have kept your leftovers cold for six days and you still open me hoping for a miracle. There is no miracle. There is hummus.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes this single line — 131 characters, no trailing newline — to `/output/entry.txt`, deterministically. Two runs produce byte-identical output.

## Why this line

The fridge has guarded the same leftovers for six days. It knows its owner isn't hungry at 3am — they're hoping. The punchline lands on the most anticlimactic thing a fridge can confess to holding: hummus. No milk, no ice, no door left open — just a tired machine telling the truth.
